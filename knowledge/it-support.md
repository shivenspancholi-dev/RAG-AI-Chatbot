# IT Support Guide

This is a sample document for the demo knowledge base of the fictional company Northwind Studio.

## Resetting your password

To reset your password, open the self-service portal and choose "Forgot password". A reset link is sent to your recovery email address and is valid for 30 minutes. Passwords need at least 12 characters, including one number and one symbol.

Passwords expire every 180 days. You receive a reminder email 7 days before your password expires.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication is required for email and the VPN. Use the authenticator app on your phone. If you lose your phone, contact IT support to register a new device.

## Getting help

Raise a ticket in the help desk portal for any IT issue. Urgent problems, such as a lost laptop, can be reported by phone to the IT support line between 08:00 and 20:00 on working days. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 24 hours.
