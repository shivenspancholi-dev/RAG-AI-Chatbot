# Expense Policy

This is a sample document for the demo knowledge base of the fictional company Northwind Studio.

## Submitting expense claims

Submit expense claims through the finance portal within 30 days of the purchase. Attach a photo or scan of every receipt. Claims submitted after 30 days need approval from the finance manager.

## Approval and reimbursement

Your manager approves claims under 500. Claims of 500 or more also need approval from finance. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel desk. Economy class is used for flights under six hours. The daily meal allowance during business travel is 40 per day.

## Non-reimbursable items

Personal entertainment, fines, and upgrades that were not approved in advance are not reimbursed.
