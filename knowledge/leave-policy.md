# Leave Policy

This is a sample document for the demo knowledge base of the fictional company Northwind Studio. Replace it with your own documents.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at two days per month. Part-time employees receive annual leave in proportion to their contracted hours.

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be used before 31 March, after which they expire.

## Requesting leave

Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day of leave. Requests of three days or fewer need one week of notice. Your manager approves or declines the request within three working days.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than two consecutive days, a medical certificate is required. Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence.

## Public holidays

The company observes the national public holidays published at the start of each year. If you work on a public holiday at your manager's request, you receive a replacement day off.
