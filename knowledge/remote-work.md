# Remote Work Policy

This is a sample document for the demo knowledge base of the fictional company Northwind Studio.

## Eligibility

Employees who have completed their three-month probation period can work remotely up to three days per week. Roles that need on-site equipment are excluded and are listed in the HR portal.

## Core hours

Remote employees are reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Outside core hours you can arrange your schedule freely, as long as your team agrees.

## Equipment and security

The company provides a laptop and a monitor for home use. Connect to company systems only through the VPN. Do not work on confidential documents on public Wi-Fi without the VPN switched on.

## Working from another country

Working remotely from another country for more than 14 days needs written approval from HR, because of tax and insurance rules.
